Building materials

What do we stock?

From structural materials to paint, in one warehouse.

  • Cement and mortar

    Cement, ready mortar, sand and gravel; bags and pallets.

  • Rebar and profiles

    Rebar, mesh, profiles and tie wire.

  • Bricks, blocks and insulation

    Wall materials plus thermal and waterproofing products.

  • Paint and plaster

    Interior and exterior paint, plaster and filler.

  • Hand tools and hardware

    Drill bits, screws, anchors and hand tools.

  • Delivery to site

    Planned delivery based on address and vehicle access.
